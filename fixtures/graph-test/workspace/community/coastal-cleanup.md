# Barangay Wawa Coastal Cleanup

The barangay council invites residents and student volunteers to a shoreline cleanup
on October 18, 2026, starting 6:00 AM at the Wawa fish port.

Plastic waste and household discharge near the fishpens lower water quality in
Laguna de Bay and contribute to fish kills during the dry season.

Thesis volunteers will take extra water samples at the cleanup points listed in
[Sampling Sites](../thesis/sampling-sites.md).
