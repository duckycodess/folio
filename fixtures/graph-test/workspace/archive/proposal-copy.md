# Thesis Proposal: Water Quality of Laguna de Bay near Barangay Wawa

## Objectives

This study measures dissolved oxygen, pH, turbidity, and nitrate levels along the
Binangonan shoreline of Laguna de Bay, focusing on Barangay Wawa where fishpens and
household discharge meet.

The final defense is scheduled for October 24, 2026. The total requested budget is
PHP 12,500, covering test kits, transport, and printing.
