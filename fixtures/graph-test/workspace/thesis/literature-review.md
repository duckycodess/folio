# Literature Review

Laguna de Bay is the largest lake in the Philippines and supplies fish and water to
Metro Manila and nearby provinces. Earlier monitoring by the Laguna Lake Development
Authority reported low dissolved oxygen near fishpen areas during the dry season.

Studies of shoreline communities found that untreated household discharge raises
nitrate levels and turbidity, which can trigger algal blooms and fish kills.

Most studies sampled the lake center. Our proposal fills the gap by sampling the
Binangonan shoreline. See the [proposal objectives](proposal.md#objectives).
