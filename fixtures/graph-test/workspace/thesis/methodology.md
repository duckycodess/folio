# Methodology

We collect water samples from six sampling sites listed in
[Sampling Sites](sampling-sites.md) every Saturday morning between 6:00 and 8:00 AM.

Each sample is tested on site for dissolved oxygen and pH using a portable meter.
Turbidity is read with a Secchi disk. Nitrate is measured with colorimetric test kits
back at the campus laboratory within four hours of collection.

Data collection starts on October 10, 2026 and runs for two weeks, ending before the
defense on October 24, 2026. Readings are logged in a shared spreadsheet and checked
by a second member of the group.

The approach follows the monitoring practice described in the
[Literature Review](literature-review.md).
